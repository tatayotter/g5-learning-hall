# Portal sprite

Source: [Portals 32 x 48](https://opengameart.org/content/portals-32-x-48) by
RodHakGames, OpenGameArt.org.

**License**: CC0 (public domain) — free for personal and commercial use, no
attribution required.

`portalsSpriteSheet.png` is the original download: 4 color variants (blue,
red, orange, gray), 4 animation frames each, 32x48px per frame, 128x192px
total.

`portal-orange.png` is a crop of just the orange row (row 3) — 4 frames,
32x48px each, 128x48px total. This is the one actually used in the game.
